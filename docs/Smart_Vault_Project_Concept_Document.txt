1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Smart Vault is an intelligent digital management system designed to consolidate, organize, and track critical
physical and digital legal documents (property registries, vehicle insurance, rental contracts, personal
identiﬁcation) in one highly secure location. By leveraging OCR and AI indexing, it automates tagging, tracks
expiration dates, and enables natural language context searches.
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Fragmentation
Scattered Documents
Important family assets (deeds, tax receipts,
vehicle records, agreement copies) are fragmented
across physical folders, emails, and cloud drives,
making retrieve-on-demand stressful and slow.
Missed Deadlines
Unnoticed Expirations
Critical deadlines like PUC renewals, vehicle
insurance expirations, and rent agreement renewals
are forgotten due to a lack of active monitoring
systems.
Emergency Vulnerability
Lack of Family Access
In cases of medical emergencies or unexpected
events, family members often have no visibility or
legal access to key documents, deeds, or asset
records.
Digital Lock-in
Limitations of Current Apps
Existing apps like DigiLocker only store
government-issued identity cards and fail to
structure private contracts, bills, deeds, and asset
histories.
3. PROPOSED SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE
Smart Vault bridges the gap between passive storage and active asset management by creating an automated
hierarchy for every physical asset:
Auto-Extraction (OCR): Simply snap a photo; AI extracts numbers, dates, and names automatically.
Asset Grouping: Organizes documents around assets (e.g., "Flat No. 302" holds registry, electricity bills, tax
receipts, and tenant agreements).
Proactive Alerting: Automated notiﬁcation engine for impending document expirations and renewals.

6. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
Phase 1 (MVP): User authentication, ﬁle upload to encrypted storage, and manual categorization (Property,
Vehicle, Identity).
Phase 2 (AI Integration): OCR integration for automatic document type recognition, date extraction, and
renewal notiﬁcations.
Phase 3 (Smart Search & Emergency Access): Natural language search inside document contents and
conﬁguration of emergency family nominee access triggers.
